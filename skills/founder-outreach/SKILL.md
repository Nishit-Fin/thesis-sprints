# Founder outreach

## Steps

1. **Shortlist in.** Take the startups from the thesis, with the one reason each
   one is on the list. That reason becomes the first line of the email.
2. **One link per founder.** Create a one-time Calendly link for each founder
   (Calendly "single-use scheduling link"), so every booking can be traced to
   one email and no link gets reused.
3. **Draft, never send.** Put each email in Gmail as a draft. Short: why I am
   writing, what I want to learn, the link. I read and send every one myself.
4. **Before the call.** When a call is booked, pull the booking and any answers
   the founder gave in the booking form. Build a question bank from the thesis's
   open questions for that company: team, proof, customers, money, risks. Ten
   questions at most, most important first.
5. **After the call.** Write the answers into the thesis doc under that company,
   marked "founder says", with the date.

## Rules

- No attachments or claims about who I work for unless the user says so.
- If a founder has not replied in 7 days, draft one follow-up. Only one.
- Plain words. No em dashes.
